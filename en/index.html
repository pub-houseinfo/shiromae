FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is from 15:00 to 19:00 and check-out is at 11:00 the following morning. You check yourself in on the tablet in the shared lobby, and smart locks mean there are no keys to hand over.
Is there parking?
We have no dedicated parking. If you are arriving by car, please use one of the nearby pay car parks; we will send details of convenient options around Himeji Station in advance.
How do I book the samurai experience at Yumeorikan?
Please book directly with Yumeorikan, the period-costume studio on the ground floor of the same building. Guests of BESSO SHIROMAE receive a special discount, and full details are also shown on the check-in tablet.
Can we stay with children?
Hideyoshi on the second floor sleeps four and includes a tatami room, making it ideal for families. Please note that towels are not provided for infants sharing a bed, so do bring your own.
Is there language support?
This site is available in four languages — Japanese, English, Korean and Traditional Chinese — selectable from the language switch at the top right of the screen. The check-in tablet is multilingual as well.
What is the cancellation policy?
The cancellation policy of the booking site you reserved through applies. Please note that it is strictly enforced on peak dates such as the cherry blossom season and the autumn festivals.
